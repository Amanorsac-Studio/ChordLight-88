CHORDLIGHT 88 2.0.5
Amanorsac Studio · amanorsac.studio


WHAT THIS IS

An 88-key MIDI display that lights the notes you play, names the chord,
and reads the Nashville number in your key — while your DAW keeps the
same keyboard. Three sounds are built in, so every clip has music.


INSTALLING

Windows    Run Chordlight88-2.0.5-Windows.exe and follow the installer.
macOS      Open Chordlight88-2.0.5-macOS.dmg and drag Chordlight 88 to
           Applications.

Free trial  Chordlight88FreeTrial-2.0.5-Windows.exe / -macOS.dmg installs
            beside the full version: 7 days, recordings up to 1 minute.


WHAT GETS INSTALLED

Standalone    C:\Program Files\Amanorsac Studio\Chordlight 88\
              /Applications/Chordlight 88.app

The installer shows this path and lets you change it.


YOUR SETTINGS AND RECORDINGS

Windows    Documents\Amanorsac Studio\Chordlight 88\
macOS      ~/Documents/Amanorsac Studio/Chordlight 88/

Uninstalling does not delete these. The free trial uses the same folder,
so the full version picks up your settings and recordings.


YOUR LICENCE KEY

One key, two computers. The first time you open Chordlight it asks for the
key on your My Apps page at amanorsac.studio. After that it works offline
and checks in again whenever it can. About > Deactivate this computer frees
the seat for another machine.

The Free Trial edition needs no key: seven days, a minute at a time, and it
never connects to the internet.


SOUND

Own · Grand · SP · EP on the title bar: your own instrument, or one of
the built-in sounds — heard through the computer and recorded into every
clip. System sound capture ("what is playing") is available on Windows;
on a Mac use the built-in sounds or an audio input.


IF IT DOES NOT SEE YOUR KEYBOARD

1. Open Setup and check MIDI input. "All inputs" hears every port.
2. Chordlight never takes a port for itself, so leave your DAW and
   Kontakt open — they all hear the same keyboard.
3. A controller with an exclusive-access driver can be shared through
   loopMIDI.

Still missing? hello@amanorsac.studio


UNINSTALLING

Windows    Settings > Apps > Chordlight 88 > Uninstall
macOS      Drag /Applications/Chordlight 88.app to the Bin, then delete
           ~/Library/Application Support/Amanorsac Studio/Chordlight 88/
           if you want the window positions gone too.
